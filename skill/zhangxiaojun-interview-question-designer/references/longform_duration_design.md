# Long-Form Duration Design

Use this reference when deciding interview length and pacing.

## Duration Baseline

张小珺式深访不是短问答。公开节目线索显示，这类节目常以长对话为核心：Apple Podcasts、Listen Notes、Podtail 等节目页都将《张小珺商业访谈录》描述为“2至7小时的长对话”；节目列表和标题中也常见“三小时”“四小时”这样的表达。已处理的罗福莉访谈本地样本约 3 小时 34 分钟。

Practical default:

- `120 min`: compressed deep interview, suitable for public release cut or busy guest.
- `180 min`: default deep interview, enough to cover background, mechanism, decisions, organization, and future.
- `240 min`: technical founder/researcher/CEO, suitable for AI, hard tech, large product strategy, or company inflection stories.
- `360 min`: archive-level conversation, suitable for second interview, major turning point, or documentary preparation.

Shorter 30/60-minute plans can be offered as editing cuts, not as the primary design.

## 180-Minute Default Arc

```text
0-12 min     Opening: latest change, central contradiction, why this guest matters now
12-35 min    Personal inflection and timeline
35-70 min    Technical/product/market mechanism
70-100 min   Concrete decisions, wrong turns, tradeoffs
100-125 min  Organization, talent, resource allocation
125-150 min  Competition, industry structure, external skepticism
150-170 min  Future signals, non-consensus judgments
170-180 min  Personal reflection and closing
```

## 240-Minute Technical Arc

```text
0-15 min     Opening and core thesis
15-45 min    Timeline, taste formation, early signals
45-90 min    Deep technical/product mechanism
90-125 min   Experiments, failures, and decision logs
125-155 min  User reality, commercialization, distribution
155-185 min  Organization, hiring, incentives, compute/capital allocation
185-215 min  Competitive landscape, risks, controversies
215-235 min  Future scenarios and falsifiable signals
235-240 min  Closing reflection
```

## 360-Minute Archive Arc

Use when the goal is to create a definitive record:

1. Origin story and worldview.
2. Early career and taste formation.
3. First major inflection.
4. Technical/product mechanism.
5. Company or project operating system.
6. Hard decisions and mistakes.
7. Organization and people.
8. Industry structure.
9. Personal philosophy under pressure.
10. Future judgment and open questions.

## Pacing Rules

- Plan more questions than needed, but mark `必问`, `可删`, and `只在对方打开时追`.
- Every 20-30 minutes, shift levels: episode -> mechanism -> tradeoff -> consequence.
- Put the most technical block after trust is built, not in the first five minutes.
- Put the hardest questions after the guest has had room to explain their worldview.
- Preserve 10-20 minutes at the end for synthesis; long interviews often produce the best answers after the guest has warmed up.
