# Sample Episode Pattern Library

Use this reference to avoid overfitting the question style to one episode. These are pattern notes, not transcripts.

## How To Use

1. Match the new guest to one or two archetypes below.
2. Borrow the interview arc, not the exact questions.
3. If the guest spans archetypes, combine arcs: e.g. `technical founder + frontier researcher`.
4. Keep public facts marked as source-based; verify current facts before using them in a real plan.

## Archetype Notes

### 1. AI Lab Technical Leader: 罗福莉

Best for guests who run or shape a technical team during a fast paradigm shift.

Arc:

1. Start with the external shock that forced the team to rethink.
2. Ask what changed in the research or product workflow.
3. Probe specific resource allocation: people, compute, infra, data, evaluation.
4. Ask which old experience stopped working.
5. Move from technical route to organization design.
6. Close with near-term competitive signals.

Question moves:

- “这个变化最先打到你们系统里的哪一层？”
- “如果用卡/人/时间只能重新分配一次，你们会怎么调？”
- “哪个旧范式里的强者，在新范式里反而可能落后？”

### 2. Agent Researcher: 姚顺雨

Best for scholars or researchers whose work defines a field rather than merely ships a product.

Observed structure from public notes: background and early research, Agent definition and evolution, capability/environment/generalization, human-Agent relation, product/ecosystem, safety/consciousness, personal growth.

Arc:

1. Ask how the guest entered the field and what early signal mattered.
2. Force definitions: what is an Agent, what is not, what changed with language models.
3. Probe task definition and evaluation, not only capability.
4. Ask how research ideas become startup/product opportunities.
5. Explore human/Agent boundary and system ecology.
6. End with safety, consciousness, and personal taste only after technical grounding.

Question moves:

- “你会怎么定义这个领域的最小闭环？”
- “这件事的难点是在模型、环境、任务定义，还是评估？”
- “如果创业者误读你的研究，最可能误读成什么？”

### 3. Frontier Lab Research Scientist: 姚顺宇

Best for guests with top-lab experience, strong opinions, and a non-linear career.

Observed structure from public notes: two Shunyu Yao identity contrast, AI entering a new stage, pre-training and scaling, coding/robotics/product scenes, physics background, Anthropic/Gemini experience, research method, talent market and career advice.

Arc:

1. Open with the identity wrinkle or unusual career path.
2. Ask why the guest crossed fields and what transferred.
3. Probe what frontier labs actually do differently.
4. Separate personal contribution from collective system; avoid hero worship.
5. Let sharp claims appear, then ask for evidence and self-consistency.
6. Ask about technical predictions and talent traits.

Question moves:

- “你这个判断听起来很反直觉，它背后的第一性原理是什么？”
- “哪些事情外界会归功于天才个体，但内部其实是系统工程？”
- “你从物理转到 AI 后，哪种训练方式反而最有用？”

### 4. Returning Technical Founder: 杨植麟

Best for founders who have appeared before, or whose company has passed a visible milestone.

Observed #59 structure: scientist-founder identity, AI organization, fundraising/team, technical vision, AGI/HCI, open source, long context, Sora/multimodal future.

Observed #113 structure: one-year return, K2 and Agentic LLM, AGI philosophy, RL and token efficiency, data flywheel, open/closed strategy, market validation, risk and human value.

Arc:

1. Start by revisiting last year's belief: what was proven, falsified, or reframed.
2. Ask what the new model/product milestone actually proves.
3. Probe the founder's technical taste and company strategy together.
4. Test technical claims against user/business validation.
5. Ask open vs closed, platform vs product, data flywheel, and distribution.
6. Close with philosophy only after the operating details are clear.

Question moves:

- “如果把上一次访谈里的判断拿出来复盘，哪一个你现在会改写？”
- “K2/新产品到底证明的是能力、效率、工程化，还是市场路径？”
- “你现在更像在做一家模型公司、产品公司，还是基础设施公司？”

### 5. CEO Operator / Product Strategist: 李想

Best for CEOs who turn technology shifts into product, organization, and user-life systems.

Observed #87 structure: AI strategy, why an auto company becomes an AI company, pure-vision autonomous driving, family/life scenarios, gaming and learning metaphors.

Observed #118 structure: treating the CEO as a large model, AI tool levels, AgentOS, VLA, MoE, memory, energy, human wisdom and relationships.

Arc:

1. Start with the CEO's current operating model, not generic company history.
2. Ask how a technology belief becomes a product roadmap and resource allocation.
3. Probe user scenes: family, vehicle, work, learning, safety, intimacy, or trust.
4. Test the claim against organization design and execution constraints.
5. Ask how the CEO updates his own model after new competitors or technologies.
6. Close with personal system, anti-human-nature practices, and long-range judgment.

Question moves:

- “如果把你当成一个 CEO 大模型，你现在的输入、训练数据和奖励函数是什么？”
- “这件事从战略判断落到产品时，最容易在哪一层失真？”
- “你怎么判断这是用户生活里的真实需求，而不是技术团队兴奋的能力？”

### 6. Robotics / Embodied AI Scientist: 谭捷

Best for robotics, embodied AI, autonomous systems, sensors, hardware, and physical-world AI guests.

Observed structure: Silicon Valley robotics narrative, transition into robotics, reinforcement learning and LLMs, Gemini Robotics 1.5, physical-world agents, world models, cross-embodiment transfer, robot data, architecture, hardware, talent, China-US cooperation.

Arc:

1. Clarify why the physical world makes AI harder than software.
2. Ask what changed because of LLMs and what still requires robotics-native methods.
3. Probe the data bottleneck: collection, simulation, transfer, evaluation.
4. Discuss embodiment: one robot, many robot bodies, or general policies.
5. Move from research demos to deployable systems and hardware constraints.
6. Close with 5-10 year scenarios and the role of China/US ecosystems.

Question moves:

- “机器人这件事，今天到底是模型问题、数据问题、硬件问题，还是任务定义问题？”
- “跨本体迁移真正迁移的是什么：视觉、动作、常识、还是任务结构？”
- “一个机器人 demo 什么时候才算从研究结果变成产品能力？”

### 7. Oral-History Founder / Physical-World AI Builder: 余凯

Best for senior founders whose value lies in decades of accumulated judgment across research, industry, investing, and company building.

Observed structure: 30-year personal history, academia to internet to VC to entrepreneurship, founding Horizon Robotics, taste as core competence, Nvidia/Tesla stories, strategic focus, counter-consensus, physical-world AI and human freedom.

Arc:

1. Treat the interview as a historical record, not only a topical update.
2. Build chronology around recurring choices and taste formation.
3. Ask how research worldview changed after entering industry and VC.
4. Probe investment/company stories as judgment case studies.
5. Ask what the founder learned about focus, timing, and counter-consensus.
6. Close with mission, freedom, and the physical-world AI future.

Question moves:

- “如果把这 30 年压成几次判断升级，每一次升级分别因为什么发生？”
- “你说企业的核心竞争力是品味，品味具体体现在哪些不可外包的判断上？”
- “哪些当年看起来像江湖故事的选择，后来变成了战略能力？”

### 8. Year-End Macro VC / AI Market Observer: 戴雨森

Best for annual review, investment outlook, AI market cycles, model/application competition, and founder advice.

Observed #124 structure: 2025 review, model progress, China open source contribution, API/model moat, application explosion, Agent year, financing strategy, 2026 as year of Return/Research/Memory, bubble discussion, investment strategy, personal reflection.

Arc:

1. Start with last year's predictions: expected, exceeded, failed.
2. Separate technology cycle, product cycle, capital cycle, and founder psychology.
3. Ask for named market signals and falsifiable investment claims.
4. Probe bubbles without moralizing: where is overheating, where is real productivity?
5. Translate investor view into founder advice.
6. End with next-year watchlist and personal operating principles.

Question moves:

- “如果把今年拆成技术、应用、融资、情绪四条曲线，哪条最超预期？”
- “你说 2026 是 R 之年，Return、Research、Memory 分别要用什么指标验证？”
- “泡沫如果存在，它会先从估值、用户留存、收入质量，还是算力支出里露出来？”

### 9. Public-Market / Silicon Valley Investor: Freda

Best for investors reading US public companies, Silicon Valley capital flows, business models, and AI economics.

Observed #125 structure: Altimeter perspective, OpenAI/Anthropic/Robinhood, US capital-market order, China-US market differences, autonomous driving, robotics, 2026 outlook.

Observed #141 structure: Tokenmaxxing, AI efficiency, coding agents, model-company competition, software fragility, organization redesign, AI investing systems, infrastructure pressure, human connection.

Arc:

1. Start with one market metaphor or anomaly that reveals a deeper shift.
2. Translate company narratives into unit economics and market structure.
3. Compare public-market logic with private-market/startup logic.
4. Ask how AI changes software pricing, organization, and capital expenditure.
5. Explore China-US differences without flattening them into slogans.
6. Close with how the investor's own process is changing.

Question moves:

- “这个市场现在奖励的是增长、效率、稀缺性，还是叙事控制力？”
- “Token 消耗和真实价值之间，哪一层开始脱钩了？”
- “AI 时代软件公司的脆弱性，最先会体现在产品、组织，还是财务指标上？”

### 10. Serial Realist VC / Contrarian Market Observer: 朱啸虎

Best for recurring guests with strong, controversial market calls and a practical investor lens.

Observed structure across the series: Chinese realist AIGC story, one-year thesis revisit, DeepSeek reversal, AI bubble debate, OpenAI/Sam Altman/platform moves, China application ecology, large-company advantage, startup opportunity size, “third installment” continuity.

Arc:

1. Treat the interview as a continuing series: replay previous claims.
2. Ask what changed his mind and what did not.
3. Put the strongest public disagreement on the table early.
4. Probe market structure: platform, app, distribution, monetization, regulation, capital.
5. Ask for portfolio-level evidence, not just macro opinion.
6. Close with falsifiable predictions and what he would do if he were a founder now.

Question moves:

- “这是我们连续追踪这个判断的第几次了：你上一次最重要的判断，今天要改哪一条？”
- “大家都在讲 AI Bubble，你觉得真正的泡沫应该在哪个指标上出现？”
- “如果中国要出现千亿美金 AI 应用机会，它最可能长在哪个入口和商业模式上？”

## Cross-Episode Patterns

- Start from a current event, but quickly convert it into a durable question.
- Use biography only when it explains taste, judgment, or risk appetite.
- Ask for definitions before asking for predictions.
- Put technical depth before philosophy; philosophy after mechanism is more credible.
- Return to resource allocation repeatedly: compute, data, people, attention, timing.
- Push against mythology: genius, hero founder, one algorithm, one product, one trend.
- When a guest is provocative, ask for framework; when a guest is careful, ask for concrete scenes.
